# QuickKOL legal page review

Local implementation updated on 2026-10-08. This note is internal and is not included in the website build.

The terms and privacy pages use QuickKOL branding and support@quickkol.com. Their document summary, section navigation, and reading layout draw on the following references; the text is adapted to QuickKOL rather than copied:

- https://influ-pay-website.vercel.app/pages/terms-conditions
- https://influ-pay-website.vercel.app/pages/privacy-policy

The pages cover creator discovery, public creator data, campaigns, AI output review, authorized outreach, the Chrome extension, subscriptions and credits, account controls, and privacy requests. They do not import another product's operator identity, address, escrow obligations, or jurisdiction-specific guarantees.

## Evidence in the current code

- The landing site stores language and theme locally.
- Campaign previews simulate progress and do not send messages or create purchases.
- Demo file selection displays selected files without uploading them.
- The contact form prepares a mailto draft; the user sends it in their mail application.
- Voice input uses the browser speech recognition feature.
- Workspace source includes payment, creator records, monitoring, and email authorization flows. Source code alone does not establish production data processing arrangements.

## Confirm before public legal approval

- Required contracting party and data controller identity disclosures, applicable jurisdiction, and contact details. Brand-only presentation does not resolve statutory identification requirements.
- Actual service providers, AI data use and training terms, hosting regions, transfer safeguards, and controller/processor roles.
- Information collected in production, lawful bases, cookies or analytics, retention schedules, deletion procedures, backups, and request response responsibilities.
- Published extension permissions and whether extension data handling matches the deployed implementation.
- Final renewal, cancellation, refund, plan-change, credit expiry, and tax conditions in checkout and billing controls.
- Translation accuracy in each language, including the six machine-assisted translations.

These local pages are reviewable product-specific drafts, not a confirmation of legal compliance. They do not assert unverified vendor names, storage locations, encryption measures, or fixed deletion deadlines.
