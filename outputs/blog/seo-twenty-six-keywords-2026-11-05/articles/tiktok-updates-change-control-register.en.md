# TikTok Updates: Keep a Change Control Register

Status: local review draft; confirm the publication date before release.

Track TikTok updates through official announcements, eligibility, actual account verification and operational changes.

![A social producer reviews an update log with several colored notes.](../public/assets/blog/tiktok-updates-change-control-register.jpg)

When tracking TikTok updates, save the official announcement date, markets, rollout wording and affected product. Verify on the actual account, device and permissions the team will use. Distinguish an announcement, a visible control, a successful workflow test and a change in the team’s instructions. TikTok’s September 2026 comment announcement, for example, described different rollout states for polls, voice and photos. Use the verified workflow for the campaign and keep a fallback when access is unavailable.

## Identify which work the update affects

Replace a general note about new features with a verifiable task. Can a creator use a comment poll? Does an editor need a new report? Does a LIVE setup have another control? Name the accounts, formats, markets and project dates affected. Use [a social media operating plan](https://www.quickkol.com/blog/social-media-marketing-operating-plan/#answer) to assign feature checks, content changes, permissions and service work. Keep unrelated announcements in a research log. One widely shared post need not change the whole workflow. For an Ads Manager or monetization update, check the documentation for that product before relying on experience from the regular app. Suppose a policy notice concerns paid distribution while an editor handles only organic posts. The media owner can review its effect on ads, and the editor can keep a narrower note on what is relevant to their work. Set a decision date based on the project schedule, not the size of the announcement. A short record with one task and owner is easier to verify than a long collection of headlines.

## Separate the announcement date from rollout status

The September 3, 2026 [TikTok comment features announcement](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/say-more-in-the-comments?lang=en) described Comment Polls, Voice Comments, photo carousels and Live Photo comments. At the time, TikTok described Comment Polls as globally available and Voice Comments for adults 18 and older as rolling out globally over the following month. The photo features also had different wording. This records the announcement’s statements at that date. It does not establish visibility on every account today. If a brand wants a poll, have the actual publisher check the relevant post and available control. In the project plan, identify the account checked and date. A product announcement can be the first source, while a later help page may describe the supported workflow more precisely. Keep both with their dates if they answer different questions. If wording differs, follow the current source relevant to the account and preserve the previous claim as historical context. Do not update a public how-to from the announcement alone when the actual control has not been reviewed.

## Set an account-specific acceptance check

Record the surface, account type, market, device, app version, date and role. Inspect the approved interface and distinguish a visible control from completion of the intended action. A successful test in one account does not establish access for another. Use an authorized test environment where possible and leave public posting with the authorized owner. If the control is available for comments on the creator’s own video, write that scope rather than assuming it appears on other people’s posts. If it is missing, note the actual step and the official support route. Suppose a producer sees the control while an editor with another account does not. Record both conditions and avoid describing the difference as a universal fault. The account owner can determine whether to wait, use the fallback or check settings. A review should state what was actually tested. A screenshot of a menu is enough to confirm that menu appeared, while the result of a workflow has to be checked separately.

## Review reporting and policy changes at the field level

[TikTok ad video play definitions](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/video-play?lang=en-0) specify starts, two-second, six-second and focused-view measures. If an export renames or adds a field, keep the old and new labels and compare the same campaign and period. Use the [campaign measurement guide](https://www.quickkol.com/blog/creator-campaign-measurement/#answer) to record affected reports, formulas and downstream owners. For a policy change, note effective date, market, account and content scope. Before joining an old view series to a new definition, confirm the compatibility of their units. If documentation is unclear, mark the conclusion pending and preserve the observations. Suppose the company sees a lower number after changing a dashboard column. Check whether it moved from starts to a duration-based measure before revising a performance claim. If historical data cannot be recalculated, start a new series and explain the break. Give finance or account teams the narrowest confirmed effect. A change in wording may leave reporting intact, while a change in counting can affect the pilot’s success criterion. Document what you can establish from the official definition and authorized export.

## Plan a fallback for unavailable features

Suppose a brand plans to use a comment poll to select its next demonstration. If the publishing account cannot confirm access, use an approved public question and summarize responses within the agreed scope, or wait for the account check. The substitute has a different collection method. Update the brief and success criteria accordingly. For commercial content, use [TikTok commercial disclosure guidance](https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-and-business-accounts/promoting-a-brand-product-or-service) to verify the actual format and relationship. Record who clears the substitute, who updates the destination and when the team can return to the original plan. Avoid requesting unnecessary personal information to participate. If a later rollout makes the poll available, first run the account-specific check and confirm the report scope. A brand might still choose the public question because people need space to explain their answer. The handoff should show the actual option used and why. If two teams use different options across markets, report each response unit separately instead of pooling the counts as one poll.

## Give the update register usable states and owners

Use separate register states for announcement read, eligibility checked, account workflow verified and team instructions updated. Give each change a source, date, owner, affected document and next review. Allow an out-of-scope or unresolved state. Split one announcement into rows when features have different conditions. A team can decide not to use a feature even when it is available. Record the actual reason and evidence. After verification, have the authorized project owners update the script and reporting instructions through their normal workflow. Suppose a new comment format changes what moderators must review. Ask the moderation owner to check staffing and handoff before making it part of a launch. The updated entry should point to the confirmed workflow and the fallback. That lets a later producer choose the right route without relying on a stale chat. Review the register periodically and retire old instructions after checking whether any active campaign still depends on them.

## Review the changed instructions in context

Check the actual release copy, captions, destination and reporting instructions together. If early copy promised a poll and the fallback invites comments, update the public question and internal tally instructions. Preserve the conditions for already published material and label the reporting scope of the revised version. Where some markets use a new feature and others are still waiting, give them separate statuses and dates. Assign a verification task, such as opening the setting in the intended account or reproducing a sample report from the correct field. If the editor must change an old help page, record who approved the correction and what evidence supports it. The team should be able to recover the instruction that applied on a specific campaign day. Keep an open issue visible rather than using a generic completed status. A successful review closes the specific task the update affected and sets a follow-up if the account or rollout changes.

## Schedule the next useful check

When an announcement describes a staged rollout, schedule the next useful check around the project deadline. Name the owner and the evidence required. If the account has not been verified, use the agreed fallback and keep the item open. Another editor should be able to find the last observation date and the next step without starting the research again.

## Rehearse a change across two markets

Suppose a team plans a comment poll in two markets. One creator has verified the control and the other has not. Record each market, account, planned version, evidence and next check. For the verified version, have the operator review settings, script and participation. For the other, prepare an approved public question and a clear way to collect answers. If the same analyst reports both markets, give votes and comment answers different fields. Do not add them as if they were the same unit. Let the respective publishing owners correct each script. If the feature becomes available later in the second market, check whether it still serves the current assignment and decide whether to switch. Preserve the reason for using the fallback at the original time. If an early screenshot becomes inaccessible, the date and description of the actual test can still help reconstruct what was known. Keep newer announcements as later records. A version note should name any audience-facing promise that changes and the person who can update it. If the campaign is already running, identify when the changed wording can take effect. An account capability can change without requiring a new creative direction. The team can continue with the tested method if it serves viewers, while leaving the feature available for a future pilot. For closeout, say which markets used each method and which results can be compared. Give the next producer a short follow-up if the relevant account or feature changes again.

## TikTok update checklist

Verify the official source and the actual account.

- [ ] Save announcement date, market and eligibility.
- [ ] Check the intended account and device.
- [ ] Distinguish control visibility and workflow verification.
- [ ] Keep a campaign fallback.
- [ ] Log revised instructions and owner.
