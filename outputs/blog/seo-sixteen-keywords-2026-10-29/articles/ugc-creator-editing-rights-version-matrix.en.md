# UGC Creator Assets: Build an Editing and Rights Version Matrix

Status: local review draft; confirm the publication date before release.

Connect UGC source files, edited versions, permissions and expiry actions in one index so later reuse remains traceable.

![ Bold type and separate version cards on a cream background emphasize one shoot and clearly managed versions. ](../public/assets/blog/ugc-creator-editing-rights-version-matrix.jpg)

QuickKOL editorial graphic; not actual account metrics or a platform interface.

A UGC creator asset handoff must answer two questions: what is the file, and how may it be used? Give source material and each derivative an identifier, then record approval, component assets, channels, territories, term and expiry ownership. A downloaded file, content approval or platform data access does not replace permission for the particular use.

## Design the handoff around the next reuse

After filming is accepted, material often travels to editors, social teams, media buyers, regional colleagues and retail partners. The person who understood the original conditions may have left the project. A folder containing final, final-two and a scattered email thread cannot reliably tell the next user which version may be advertised, whether music has expired or whether another language is permitted.

A version matrix should preserve information that changes a usage decision, not add an elaborate system to every small commission. List expected downstream uses first, then choose the necessary fields. A package intended only for an owned social account needs fewer rows than one covering regional advertising, captions and retail distribution. Both need recognizable files and a traceable permissions source.

The [paid content licensing schedule guide](/blog/influencer-content-licensing-paid-media-schedule/#answer) helps organize uses and terms. Test the handoff against an actual request: can this passage appear in a new advertisement next month? If the answer requires searching every conversation, add the missing link, owner or condition. The purpose is a reproducible decision, not a sheet whose cells remain permanently green. Record who resolves uncertainty so the next team does not treat an unanswered question as an informal approval.

## Give source and derivative files distinct identities

Distinguish source footage, clean audio, captioned masters, stills and advertising adaptations. Retain the project, creator, asset identifier, version, language, aspect ratio and export date. Filenames can stay short if the index locates the actual file. Do not overload a filename with every term, or allow renaming to destroy the connection with its source.

Record the ingredients of each derivative. An edit may combine two shoots, a brand animation and a different music track. Permission for one source clip does not establish identical conditions for the assembled video. Keeping the component relationship makes it possible to find affected versions when an ingredient is corrected, withdrawn or expires, without manually watching the whole library again.

Delivery does not necessarily include editable project files, raw footage, fonts or every audio stem. Negotiate these as explicit deliverables and check whether the recipient can open the project in its working environment. If something is unavailable, document a usable alternative export. Having a project file is not the same as being able to recreate it completely. A small index that allows a colleague to locate and understand the material is often more useful than several undocumented archives.

## Separate content approval from permission to use

Content approval establishes whether facts, expression and production meet the agreed brief. Usage approval establishes who may use a version, where, how and for how long. Different people may approve these, under different conditions. A video can be content-approved while advertising remains pending, or still licensed while its product explanation has become outdated.

The [U.S. Copyright Office overview](https://www.copyright.gov/what-is-copyright/) explains the basic protection of original expression. Actual permissions depend on applicable law and the agreement; payment or file receipt cannot independently establish unlimited use. This matrix is an operational record, not a universally applicable contract or a replacement for appropriate review of its terms.

Retain the permission document’s location, matching version and decision owner. If a permitted use is described in an email, preserve its original context rather than copying a sentence without its conditions. A pending state should lead the receiving team to a clear contact and a hold on the affected use. Unknown information is more useful when it remains visible than when a generic licensed label conceals a purpose that was never agreed. Revisit the record whenever the business changes the intended audience or placement.

## Track music and other component conditions

A creator appearing in a video does not mean they own every ingredient. Music, fonts, brand-supplied imagery, other voices and background works may have different conditions. Before acceptance, ask who supplied each relevant component, which uses are platform-specific and which ingredients need replacement.

The [TikTok Commercial Music Library guide](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-use-the-commercial-music-library?lang=en-GB) describes regional and usage-context filtering. Music available within a platform should not be generalized into unlimited permission across every platform, territory and term. If the brand plans to move the video elsewhere, check the current scope separately. An exported file containing music is not automatically a single package with every possible right cleared.

Prepare clean-dialogue and music-free versions when needed, while confirming that they belong to the agreed deliverables. Replacing a track can change pacing, caption timing and meaning, so create a new version and review it accordingly. The matrix can flag the replacement at component level and connect it to affected masters. That prevents a media team from relying on the old approval after changing a material ingredient. It also helps distinguish a technically playable file from one ready for the intended placement, particularly when an asset moves between teams or platforms.

## Check platform access separately from asset rights

Brand partner access, data sharing and advertising settings are not one permission. [YouTube brand partner access documentation](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15672082?hl=en) describes video-level sharing while reminding partners to discuss content usage separately. Show report access, completed platform settings and agreed advertising scope as separate states. Technical ability should not be mistaken for an accepted commercial use.

For each platform setting, record the account, authorized object, owner, term and withdrawal conditions without storing passwords or tokens in the matrix. Access may relate to one published video while the brand’s later uploaded advertisement is another object. Check the actual promotion method and asset version rather than copying a green state from a previous assignment.

Before activation, media confirms settings, content confirms the version, and the commercial owner confirms scope. If access is withdrawn, inspect the reporting and advertising consequences. If the asset permission expires, check what records may still be retained. These changes can happen together or independently. Keeping their states separate reduces incorrect inferences between a platform button and the parties’ commitment. It also provides a concrete handoff when a new team member takes over a campaign that is already running.

## Define when editing or translation creates a new review

Agree which changes can proceed and which need another review. A crop can remove conditions, disclosure or an important demonstration detail. Translation can alter terminology, tone and product claims. It is only a small edit is not an assessment of its actual effect. Connect the rule to the intended use rather than the number of minutes an editor spent.

For each derivative, record the change, editor, date and required approval. A changed product model needs a factual check. A promotional price needs market and timing confirmation. Turning a natural account of experience into advertising copy requires another look at expression and scope. Voice synthesis, appearance changes or other material alterations to the creator’s expression should not be concealed inside a generic editing field.

The [UGC creative testing guide](/blog/creator-ugc-testing-plan/#answer) helps plan purposeful version differences. Changing one factor can make a comparison easier, but it does not authorize an otherwise unpermitted change. Give a test version a hypothesis, use and exit condition, then return its approval to the index. This keeps experimentation from generating a collection of orphaned masters whose permitted reuse nobody can explain. Keep rejected experiments distinguishable from versions approved for publication.

## Locate placements before the permission expires

Expiry management requires knowing where an asset is used, not just when an agreement ends. Record owned posts, advertisements, websites, sales materials, regional partners and other actual distribution points. Stopping one advertisement does not necessarily end every use. Another team may have uploaded the same material or embedded it in a long-lived page.

Assign an owner and an action before expiry. Depending on the agreement, the action may involve requesting renewal, stopping new uses, replacing page material or removing particular versions. Do not impose one universal removal rule without checking the terms. Retain the decision, execution date and affected locations so the next colleague can see what is complete and what remains pending.

A renewal also needs the specific assets and scope. Internal archival retention and continued public distribution may be different questions. A new market, retail partner or advertising purpose can require another discussion. Escalate locations that cannot be handled promptly instead of marking the project closed in advance. A visible unresolved action is more useful than premature closure. Confirm completion with the people responsible for actual placements, since the central content team may not control every downstream account or page.

## Test the matrix against a realistic reuse request

Suppose a brand receives an approved vertical product demonstration for its owned social account. Two months later, a regional team wants a horizontal advertisement with a local price. This hypothetical scenario does not establish that any permission is necessarily available. The recipient first locates the original and its terms, then checks the crop, price facts, territory, advertising method and music conditions.

If cropping removes an essential demonstration, re-edit or commission another shot. If music does not suit the new use, prepare a replacement. If the scope does not cover the placement, resolve terms first. Create a new identifier after the work, linking changes, approvals, permissions and actual placements to the source. The original version keeps its own state rather than being overwritten.

Finally, ask a colleague uninvolved in the commission to identify which version can be used, who confirmed it and when it needs another check. That is a practical test of the handoff. The matrix cannot promise that mistakes never occur, but it should reveal uncertainty earlier. The brand gains manageable assets while the creator’s expression and agreed boundaries remain traceable through repeated transfers and later reuse.

## File, permission and placement matrix

| Layer | Required record | When it changes |

| --- | --- | --- |

| Source | Identifier, components, file and delivery scope | Locate derivatives |

| Edit | Changes, language, ratio and approval | Review affected content |

| Permission | User, channel, territory, term and evidence | Hold uncertain uses and contact the owner |

| Placement | Location, operator, term and action | Update or stop affected placements |

An operational index; actual rights and expiry actions depend on applicable terms.

## Asset handoff checks

Validate the record with the next reuse request.

- [ ] Sources and derivatives have locatable identifiers.

- [ ] Content approval and usage permissions are separate.

- [ ] Music and other components retain their conditions.

- [ ] Platform access and advertising settings are checked separately.

- [ ] New edits and translations have change and approval records.

- [ ] Actual placements and owners can be found before expiry.
