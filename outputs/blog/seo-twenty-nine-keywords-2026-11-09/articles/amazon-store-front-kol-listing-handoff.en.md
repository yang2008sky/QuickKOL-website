# How to Check an Amazon Storefront Partnership

Status: local review draft; confirm the publication date before release.

Follow the creator’s video through their storefront to the intended listing, variant and reporting scope.

![A creator comparing sample products on a worktable](../public/assets/blog/amazon-store-front-kol-listing-handoff.jpg)

Accept an Amazon Storefront partnership against the shopping route promised in the creator’s work. Start from the sponsored content, open the creator’s store or list and verify the marketplace, product variant and disclosure. The creator confirms their publishing link. The brand confirms the listing and any approved substitute. Report clicks, orders and commission under their respective definitions. If the route breaks, document the accepted creator work separately from the sales evidence affected by the fault.

## Confirm which shopper route the partnership includes

A creator storefront, a single product link and a brand’s seller store can point toward the same item. They differ in ownership, presentation and reporting. [Amazon.com’s storefront Idea List guide](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GU8CZSAGGW28CGMG) explains how Idea Lists appear on a creator’s storefront. If the brief purchases a recommendation in that store, specify who adds the product to which list and whether it stays there after the campaign ends. For a direct item recommendation, use the link permitted under the platform and agreement. A visit to a storefront is not the same measurement as a visit to a specific product page. Use an [Amazon storefront partnership routes guide](https://www.quickkol.com/blog/amazon-storefront-creator-brand-program-routes/#answer) to establish the program path first.

List the actual route from each surface. A creator’s video description might open the store, while a profile link goes directly to a list. The brand’s own email may go straight to a product listing. The first two involve creator distribution. The third belongs to the brand’s owned channel. For every route, record the publishing account, editing owner, disclosure location and report owner. Avoid collapsing distinct commitments into a single Amazon link field. The storefront administrator and the measurement owner need to know exactly which links they maintain.

## Verify the product and variant in the target marketplace

Ask for the model, size, color and version of the sample used on screen. Open the marketplace the customer will use and verify the listing and selected variant against the creative. Keep the ASIN or another listing identifier and the observation time. A page can group several colors and capacities. Its default selection may differ from the photographed item. A shared product family does not establish the same SKU. If the link redirects, record the final listing and actual selection rather than checking the domain alone.

Suppose the creator shows a blue 500 ml insulated cup. The storefront list contains the green 750 ml version and the detail page shows the blue option out of stock. The team can wait for stock, film an available variant or revise the promise with an approved explanation. A matching page title does not settle the discrepancy. Where the brand supports another marketplace, run the intended customer route there and verify currency and purchase availability. Record that each check is time-bound. The campaign plan needs a fallback for a later stock change, even when every link opened correctly in review.

## Walk the full route on a phone before launch

Open the actual link from a preview of the post. Check it in a phone browser and in any relevant app context for the intended market. Does it show the right store? Can a shopper find the item in the list? Does it jump to another marketplace or a different variant? A public preview or authorized test view is sufficient. The team does not need to place a test order to check a landing page. If the browser works but the app opens its home screen, record the device, time and redirect steps for the link owner.

Split the checks between two people. The creator verifies the spoken instruction, list and URL. The brand verifies specifications, availability and acceptable replacements. Keep the date and page name with each result. If a short link opens an older list, fix it at the publishing source and run the complete route again. Verify the copy still makes sense after the destination changes. A successful status code only establishes that a page was served. It does not establish that a viewer found the size or color they were asked to select.

## Check disclosures in the content and storefront

The creator may receive a sample, a production fee and affiliate commissions. Specify which relationships apply, where each disclosure appears in language the intended audience understands and who checks the final view. In the U.S. context, [the FTC’s influencer disclosure guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers) emphasizes making material connections easy to notice near the endorsement. The brand should also verify the applicable identification and link requirements in [the Amazon.com Associates operating agreement](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement). Other markets can have different program and local requirements. Open the live post on a phone and confirm that the disclosure is discoverable during ordinary viewing.

For example, a creator might present a cup and then direct viewers to a store list. Check the disclosure in the content or caption, and verify whether the program requires wording in an additional location. An affiliate connection can be relevant when there is no flat production fee. If the only wording is an unclear abbreviation buried below other text, route a correction to the owner. Attach the disclosure, final content version and approval to the same delivery record. Payment evidence does not establish that a live endorsement disclosed its commercial relationship.

## Separate clicks, orders and commissions

[Amazon.com’s Associates reports overview](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/welcome/topic/reports) lists views by tracking ID, link type, orders and earnings. [Amazon.com’s Onsite Store ID guide](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GPW5YCEUHGH83SMD) distinguishes its onsite identifier from the ID used for traffic the creator sends to Amazon. Confirm which report the creator will use, its marketplace and which tracking fields may be shared. Agree when an order or earnings value is considered ready for reporting. Have the authorized owner supply an aggregate and redact unrelated account, product and financial details. Allocate an order to an individual post only where the reporting and agreement support that allocation.

A brand’s outbound click count, affiliate clicks and completed sales may have different windows. For example, a brand link can record 90 clicks, while a creator export shows seven items ordered and five shipped. Report the values under their respective sources. The brand clicks cannot automatically be credited with the five shipments. Where reporting differs by marketplace, return status or cutoff, preserve the initial extraction time and reconciliation sheet. Explain the unit and attribution method when presenting any performance figure to the buyer.

## Plan an out-of-stock and redirect correction

Add a response for stock loss, price changes, redirects and mislinked related products to the campaign calendar. Assign an update owner, first check and criteria for pausing promotion. If one marketplace has stock and another does not, mark the affected market. When the brand replaces a model, compare the current content with the new listing. Point to the replacement only after the team has updated the content or confirmed the existing use is still accurate. Follow a [creator destination attribution process](https://www.quickkol.com/blog/link-in-bio-destination-attribution/#answer) and record separate observation windows for the old and new paths.

Suppose a product page begins selecting the 750 ml model three days after launch. The creator can remove the incorrect list entry while the brand checks whether there is still an accurate destination. If none is available, pause new distribution requests, update the relevant copy as agreed and record the affected period. After a correction, repeat the same device and market test before resuming approved distribution. Preserve the previous and revised reports. A click recorded during a broken path did not have the same opportunity to become a purchase as one recorded after the destination was fixed.

## Decide whether a link change alters the creator deliverable

Suppose a creator demonstrates a blue 500 ml cup and the brief calls for that same item in their Amazon list. The link opens a green 750 ml version. The brand checks whether the filmed variant is still available in the target market, then asks the creator which list they can edit. If only another variant is available, agree whether to correct the copy, reshoot the relevant shot or pause referrals. The existence of a product page does not resolve a mismatch between what viewers saw and what they can buy.

Keep the video, filmed variant, list entry, effective time and approver together. If the creator delivered the original post accurately and the brand later lost stock, preserve publication acceptance and record the time when the destination was affected. The next agreement can name an approved substitute, a notice period and a separate reporting window for any change.

## Keep the route evidence with settlement

Before settlement, confirm the video, list placement, availability period, disclosure and actual product variant. Separate tracking, commissions and any performance term from publication acceptance. If stock was lost during the campaign, use the log to show when it happened and what changed. Do not insert estimated sales for that period. When an external link only supplies an aggregate trend, show the scope next to the result. Check usage rights, list expiry and contact ownership instead of expanding permissions to prove that a link worked on a particular date.

In the next campaign, change one major variable. Keep the product, observation window and intended audience stable while testing whether a list or direct destination causes fewer shopper problems. Track link access and whatever verifiable buying actions the agreed data supports as distinct measures. Record which path required less upkeep and which uncertainties still need a sample. Finish with one shared handoff row for market, variant, link, disclosure and reporting source. Have both the creator and the brand acknowledge the fields they own.

## Creator storefront handoff fields

| Step | Record | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Storefront | Market, store and list link | Creator |
| Variant | Model, size, listing ID and date | Brand merchandising |
| Disclosure | Placement and final wording | Publisher and reviewer |
| Reporting | Scope, extraction time and definitions | Affiliate report owner |

Use the intended marketplace and the actual partnership terms.

## Prelaunch checklist

Use the actual shopper route and keep evidence.

- [ ] Verify the store, list, marketplace and filmed variant.
- [ ] Follow the mobile preview to the correct item.
- [ ] Check disclosure, permissions and the stock response owner.
- [ ] Agree on the source for clicks, orders and commission.
