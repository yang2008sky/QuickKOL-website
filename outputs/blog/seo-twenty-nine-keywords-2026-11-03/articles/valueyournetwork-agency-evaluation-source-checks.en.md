## Translate best into an answerable project question

State the project’s audience, market, language, channels and required work. One brand may need complete campaign operation while another needs coordination with a particular creator category. A shared agency name does not remove that difference.

Separate necessary conditions from preferences. Handling the intended market and deliverable may be necessary; a particular communication style may be a preference. Set conditions before reading promotion so familiar wording does not quietly redefine the assignment.

The [agency-scope comparison guide](/blog/influencer-agencies-scope-and-accountability/#answer) separates brand service, talent representation and production. A search involving ValueYourNetwork can begin research without establishing which service category should be purchased.

This guide does not propose a total score suitable for every brand. An explainable choice names the actual need, evidence and accepted limits. If an essential condition remains unconfirmed, further inquiry or a deferred decision is a valid result. That is more informative than selecting a provider from the most confident adjective on a landing page and only discovering the scope mismatch after a project has been scheduled.

## Check official sources without combining similar names

The [ValueYourNetwork official overview](https://www.valueyournetwork.com/) reviewed here is on valueyournetwork.com and describes campaigns involving Instagram, Facebook, X, TikTok and YouTube. That is a published scope description, not a platform-by-platform feature test or verification of data availability.

Record domain, page, review date and specific claim. Check ownership of similarly named sites, reposts and search snippets separately. A similar name should not allow staff counts, dates or promises from different pages to be assembled into a supposedly certain company profile.

If a detail such as a company date is unnecessary to the decision and cannot be consistently confirmed, omit it. A complete-looking introduction is not a reason to select whichever number best fits expectations.

Use the current official business route for subsequent contact, rechecking the actual page before sending. This guide collects no private contact details, assumes no universal ownership of similarly named material and makes no authenticity judgment about other websites. Its practical boundary is the evidence actually read and attributable to the provider being evaluated.

## Translate the published workflow into deliverables to confirm

The [ValueYourNetwork advertiser guide](https://www.valueyournetwork.com/annonceurs/) describes stages including briefing, creator matching and tracking or reporting. That helps prepare project questions without establishing that a particular package includes everything your assignment needs.

Ask who prepares the brief, when the brand confirms decisions, who contacts creators and which content or states appear in the final report. Map the company’s process language to actual deliverables and owners rather than repeat stage names.

Where organic publication, brand-owned assets and paid use coexist, define each scope. A long platform list does not automatically include every channel in every project, and reporting does not automatically have every native account field.

The [agency-or-direct contact guide](/blog/creator-agency-or-direct-outreach/#answer) helps distinguish representation from brand-side execution. If another party performs particular work, ask how responsibilities connect. The published process does not replace project-specific confirmation of roles and dependencies. Requesting this explanation also gives the provider a fair opportunity to state what is included, what requires another arrangement and which inputs the brand must supply before operation can begin.

## Read cases as displays, methods and causal claims separately

A brand name or case displayed on a website establishes that the company makes that public statement. Alone, it does not confirm a current client relationship, the entire assignment scope or an equivalent result for your project.

For a relevant case, look for objective, platform, period, objects, inputs and outcome definition. Views without a window, or a return claim without cost and attribution, should not become directly comparable performance data.

An appropriately anonymized example and method explanation can be requested without demanding another client’s confidential material. If information cannot be shared, retain it as unavailable and decide whether remaining evidence is sufficient.

Cases help explain work and learning rather than guarantee every displayed number. Conversely, lack of complete public commercial information does not establish lack of capability. Insufficient public evidence and absence of capability are different conclusions. This distinction keeps the evaluation fair while preserving the brand’s right to ask for the information genuinely necessary to judge its own assignment and avoid relying on an unsupported promise.

## Check whether reporting answers your business question

First decide what must be understood: delivery, audience understanding, suitable inquiries or a purchase observation. Then inspect offered sources and fields rather than infer complete attribution from the words detailed reporting.

The [campaign-measurement guide](/blog/creator-campaign-measurement/#answer) separates outputs, responses and business contribution. Mentioning tracking in a public workflow does not establish incremental causation. Confirm actual access, coverage and observation window.

Ask that metrics retain numerator, denominator, unit and time. Keep unknown separate from zero and estimates separate from observations. If reporting uses a provider score or monetary valuation, explain method and limits rather than portray it as a platform’s official certification.

Delivery format also affects reuse. A static summary, readable table, appropriate export and method note serve different needs. Ask what can actually be delivered instead of expanding website copy into unverified technical capability. This can be evaluated through a relevant example: another colleague should be able to identify where a reported conclusion came from without requiring access to unrelated client data or guessing which definition the agency intended.

## Assign responsibility for the actual market

The [French ministry influencer guide](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/guide-bonne-conduite-influenceurs-createurs-contenu) is a government entry point for commercial-influence guidance in France. A French-language agency query makes market applicability worth checking; this article does not extend that guidance into worldwide legal advice.

Ask who handles substantiation for product claims, commercial-relationship wording, review and release decisions in this project. Agency involvement does not remove the brand’s need to provide reliable information or make a creator responsible for evidence the brand has not supplied.

Authorized people should confirm contracts, permissions and applicable requirements for the actual assignment. This guide offers no unverified universal threshold, complete contract template or conclusion needing no further review.

For cross-border work, retain the distinction between audience markets and execution location. A French agency does not mean every audience member is in France; English content does not identify a single applicable country. Route questions requiring specialist confirmation to the appropriate owner, with enough context to explain the planned activity instead of asking for a generic approval of an agency name.

## A creator certificate is not agency-performance certification

The [ARPP certificate FAQ](https://www.arpp.org/influence-responsable/faq-certificat-influence-responsable-arpp/) explicitly describes a creator certificate, rather than that same certification for agencies, brands, agents or trainers. It concerns a defined training and knowledge-assessment scope, not guaranteed results for each collaboration.

Do not rewrite support for creator training, or certificates held by participating talent, as agency-performance certification. When a specific certificate matters, verify holder, type, scope and current information instead of inferring meaning from an icon.

Membership, awards, customer comments and educational certificates are different evidence types. Each can be recorded without automatically becoming a common quality score or regulatory approval.

This article has not confirmed a particular membership or certificate status for ValueYourNetwork and implies neither possession nor absence. It explains how to interpret such evidence when comparing providers. Keeping the distinction explicit avoids both overstating a positive credential and unfairly turning a missing badge into a negative verdict about a company’s ability to perform the proposed work.

## A hypothetical comparison: two proposals differ in scope

Suppose a brand receives two proposals. One includes creator publication and reporting; another also includes asset versions and a particular paid-use arrangement. These are fictional proposals, not quotations from ValueYourNetwork or another named provider.

Create common fields for service, production, creator compensation, use, media and other actual expenses. Unquoted means pending, not free. Compare totals and dependencies only when scope and charging bases are clear.

Record work performed by the brand as well. If one proposal relies on an internal team to prepare factual material and multiple exports, include those resources in planning. A lower external total does not necessarily mean lower total effort.

Finish with selection reasons and outstanding conditions. Candidates do not need to be identical in every field, but readers should understand which differences justify payment, which limitations are accepted and which unmet conditions prevent a decision. The comparison should remain adaptable if the project changes; it is a record of this assignment’s requirements, not a permanent public ranking of agencies.

## Hand off a record another colleague can continue

Summarize current website facts, unverified self-claims, fit with the brief and questions to ask. Preserve sources so another colleague can continue the assessment after a page or proposal changes.

Keep an inquiry specific and relevant without implying a purchase decision has already been made. QuickKOL can help interpret a brief, research relevant creators’ work and prepare outreach. This guide does not claim it verifies every service or private project result of this agency.

The result may be further discussion, more suitable evidence or no purchase yet. A bounded assessment gives a brand owner a more reviewable decision than an unexplained best-agency label, while representing fairly what public information can support.
