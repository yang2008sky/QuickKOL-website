# Shopify Collabs: Reconcile Creator Commissions

Status: local review draft; confirm the publication date before release.

Follow Shopify Collabs orders through billing and payout, including refunds and waiting states.

For Shopify Collabs reconciliation, separate attribution, estimated commission, holding period, billing and creator receipt. Verify which creator and program own a link or code, then review cancellations, full refunds, disputes and payout states from the actual orders. When a payment is waiting for the creator, review payout setup and platform records before taking any action that could duplicate the payment.

## Start from the merchant’s actual program scope

The [Shopify Collabs program setup guide](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/collabs/merchants/collabs-programs) describes open access and invite programs. Eligible creators may obtain links under open access. An invite program is for selected creators. The guide also describes market eligibility for open access. Check the actual store configuration, available program, products, regions, current rates and owners. If a brand needs to review product language before a creator publishes, examine the real process for the selected relationship. Use the [campaign brief guide](https://www.quickkol.com/blog/write-a-useful-campaign-brief/#answer) to document the product, window and separately agreed production or posting work. A small apparel store might test one collection with two creators before considering another product line. List which SKUs belong to the pilot and which purchases should be excluded under the actual program. Confirm which country and currency the customer will encounter. A store can have several offers that appear similar to a customer while using different commission rules. Store the version and start date so finance can reconcile orders placed before and after a program change.

## Join links, codes and actual order IDs

The [Shopify Collabs merchant payment guide](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/collabs/merchants/payments) explains that creators can earn commission through tracked affiliate links or discount codes. For each test case, record the creator, program, link or code, product, order identifier, time, currency and report source. A link click and code use at checkout are distinct events. Verify commission from the actual order record. If one order has evidence of both routes, do not count it twice. Put a deduplication rule and an unknown classification in the reporting notes. Suppose a buyer first opens a link on a phone and later uses a code on a desktop. The brand should not invent a cross-device attribution sequence from a final order row. Check what each system actually associates with that transaction. If a code appears in two places, preserve the intended creator and actual platform attribution while investigating any mismatch. A customer’s billing information is not required for a broad campaign recap. Give reviewers the necessary order reference and restrict the detailed record to authorized staff.

## Use the holding period to handle cancellations and full refunds

The [Shopify Collabs merchant payment guide](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/collabs/merchants/payments) describes a default thirty-day holding period that merchants can configure. Canceled and fully refunded orders have commissions canceled during that period under the documented process. For a partial refund, later change or price adjustment, inspect actual records rather than applying the full-refund result. Preserve order reference, refund time, amount and observed status. Suppose the eligible sales amount is 100 dollars and the applicable rate is ten percent. The initial commission estimate is ten dollars. If the order receives a full refund during the hold, record the resulting canceled status from the platform. Do not replace the original transaction value with today’s price. A store analyst can keep the order row and cancellation row linked so a later export can be reconciled. Check which timestamp the report uses before counting a refund in a monthly total. An order created near a month end might settle after the next close. A separate pending category makes the finance result easier to interpret.

## Distinguish billing from creator receipt

The [Shopify Collabs merchant payment guide](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/collabs/merchants/payments) describes processing through the Shopify bill and the need for creators to activate automatic payouts. It identifies a Waiting for creator state and warns that paying that commission outside Collabs could create a duplicate payment. Record estimated, held, billed, bill paid and receipt confirmation as separate states. If a creator reports that funds have not arrived, give finance the relevant bill and status and direct the creator to their official payout setup. Do not collect banking credentials in messages. In one hypothetical case, the merchant pays a monthly bill containing the commission, while the creator has yet to finish payout setup. The merchant can confirm the bill payment without asserting the recipient has received it. If the creator later activates payouts, keep the original status and append a dated observation. A separate production fee should be labeled and matched to its own contract. That prevents someone reading the records from assuming that a bank transfer settled the commission as well.

## Separate fixed fees, commission and processing charges

Use the [creator rates and usage rights guide](https://www.quickkol.com/blog/creator-rates-usage-rights/#answer) to separate production, permission, publication and commission. Suppose the collaboration includes a 200-dollar production fee and commission on eligible orders. The fixed fee follows its own acceptance conditions. It does not belong in the commission hold. Record processing charges, conversion and other billing lines from the relevant statements. Review what the brand paid, what the creator can verify receiving and whether the records share a period. A customer’s final checkout total cannot establish the eligible sales base by itself. Discounts, returns and the program’s configured scope can matter. In a reconciliation workbook, give each payment type a separate reference and use a common currency only after documenting the conversion. Keep original currency and value for finance review. If a marketing team measures project cost, state which charges it includes. A sales report for the creator program may use a narrower denominator than a brand margin analysis. Both can be valid if the definitions are visible.

## Open a dispute only for a specific documented issue

The [Shopify Collabs merchant payment guide](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/collabs/merchants/payments) describes commission disputes during the holding period. Verify the order, program, date, code and evidence before an authorized owner uses the applicable process. Unusual volume may justify review. It does not establish wrongdoing. Record the affected orders, submission time, response and decision. Append new evidence rather than overwriting the original observation. Keep the distinction between a dispute, a return and any decision to pause the relationship. For example, a discount code might appear on an unplanned page while orders still look ordinary in the storefront. Record where the code was actually observed and check the terms that apply. Do not assign responsibility for the publication without evidence. If a claim was mistaken, preserve the correction so finance does not hold an unrelated payment. Give the creator or representative the information they need to respond through the appropriate channel, while excluding other customers’ private details. Set a review date for the case.

## Reconcile four hypothetical orders

Suppose four orders each start with an eligible sales amount of 100 dollars and an estimated commission of ten dollars. The first is fully refunded during the hold, the second remains in the hold, the third appears on a bill and the fourth is waiting for the creator to activate payouts. The reconciliation can show forty dollars originally estimated, ten canceled and ten in each of three open states. It cannot say that creators received thirty dollars. If the third bill is later paid, confirm the payment scope and update the date. Keep all four order identifiers and source records so someone can reproduce the calculation. In a real close, add any actual fees and currency differences to the billing side rather than to the creator estimate. If the team receives an updated platform export with a new state, preserve both observation dates. Finance can then answer why an earlier campaign report and a later ledger show different totals. The difference may be a normal change in settlement state.

## Assign owners and cutoffs for the next close

Give operations the actual program and codes, finance the orders, bills and settlement states, and the creator owner any payout or permission follow-up. Agree on a monthly cutoff and carry open orders forward. An estimate does not close a payment. When the holding period, rates or eligible products change, record the effective scope. Start the next batch with the same record fields and confirm the real configuration. If someone asks for a quick campaign total, show both the current attributed sales scope and the status of commissions. Tell the reader when the open cases will be reviewed. A short summary can report the verified amount on the merchant’s bill separately from the confirmed creator receipt. Record who approved the close and which records were unavailable at that time. This helps preserve a stable accounting history while letting the marketing team see the current operating picture.

## Reconcile orders across a program change

Suppose a brand changes a collection’s commission at the end of October. Document when the old configuration applied, when the new one took effect and which record the bill actually used. Review an order on each side of the change and one refund across the close. If an order used an older link, check its recorded attribution and commission before applying a current rate. Leave a dated rule and owner for later review. If the report cannot distinguish the version automatically, make that limitation visible and retain the verified export. A brand that invites a creator into a different program may need to update the campaign brief and links as well as finance’s setup. Test both before the next promotion. The reviewer should be able to understand the old and new lines without guessing from the current dashboard or silently reclassifying an order after the monthly close.

- [ ] Record holds, refunds and disputes
- [ ] Separate billing and receipt
- [ ] Track open orders and next action
